Return the showcase to an unbranded developer harness. The Mapsight marks, brand fonts and tokens, the KERN citizen-service scenario and the operator's imprint and privacy pages are removed; the curated, branded showcase is now built separately from the published packages and served at showcase.open-mapsight.org. Latest `main` is published at preview.open-mapsight.org, and the footer links to the project website's legal notice and privacy policy. Technical demos, the icon editor, the count aggregator, the fictional demo fixtures and the static search hygiene are unchanged.
