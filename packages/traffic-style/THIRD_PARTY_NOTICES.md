The registered `fa-restroom+fa-wheelchair` icon combines the existing
`fa-restroom` and `fa-wheelchair` glyphs in the optional Font Awesome pack.
Mapsight scales, positions and colors these glyphs inside its own shared marker
frame. Generated modules and composed SVGs retain attribution to Font Awesome.
